TC Playback: layout and look options

These are static mockups to pick a direction from. The real app draws the clock, meters, scope and waveform live. The playlist content is example data. All four iPad layouts use the same parts (clock, cue list, waveform, scope, meters, L/R channel strips, transport); what changes is how much space each part gets.

Layout A: Console (current build)

Cue list on the left and the show on the right, like QLab or a broadcast playout system. It's the best all-rounder: everything is visible, nothing is hidden behind a tap.

Layout B: Clock forward

The clock takes the full width at the top, readable from across the video world. Cues run as a horizontal strip (previous, playing, standby, next), and the list is a drawer. Best when the iPad sits on a desk as a timecode display that also plays the show.

Layout C: Mixer

Built around the two channels. Tall L/R strips with meters and big Play/Mute keys sit on the left, like a console fader bank. Clock and scope are in the middle, cue list on the right. Best if you're constantly muting, swapping or checking timecode health.

Clock styles and colours

Settings: three clock faces and five preset colours, plus any custom colour. The digits dim and turn amber on freewheel, and flash red when the signal is lost.

Overall look

Four app-wide themes. Graphite is the default. True Black saves battery on OLED iPads and is the darkest at FOH. Light is for daylight and outdoor rehearsals.
